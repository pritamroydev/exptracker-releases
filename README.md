## About

ExpTracker brings your everyday financial activities together in one place.

From tracking daily expenses and income to managing accounts, credit cards, budgets, lending, and borrowing, ExpTracker helps you maintain a clearer picture of your finances.

Built with a local-first approach, your financial records are maintained on your device. No bank account integration is required.

## Features

### Financial Management
- Record income and expenses.
- Manage multiple financial accounts.
- Transfer money between accounts.
- Monitor account balances.
- Maintain a detailed transaction history.

### Credit Card Management
- Manage credit card accounts.
- Track purchases and outstanding balances.
- Record credit card bill payments.
- Monitor credit utilisation.

### Lending & Borrowing
- Track money lent to others.
- Maintain records of borrowed money.
- Record repayments.
- Monitor outstanding receivables and liabilities.

### Budgets & Insights
- Create monthly budgets.
- Monitor spending against budget limits.
- View financial summaries.
- Analyse spending patterns.

### Recurring Transactions
- Manage recurring monthly transactions.
- Receive local notifications for scheduled transactions.

### Backup & Export
- Create encrypted backups of financial data.
- Restore from supported backup files.
- Export transaction records to CSV.

### Automatic Updates
- Automatically check for new releases on app launch.
- Receive an update dialog when a newer version is available.
- View release notes before updating.
- Download APK updates directly from GitHub Releases.
- Verify downloaded APK integrity using SHA-256 when available.
- Install updates through Android's package installer.
- Manually check for updates from Settings.

## Privacy & Data

ExpTracker follows a local-first approach to personal finance management.

- Financial records are stored locally on your device.
- No bank account connection is required.
- Transactions are manually recorded.
- Users control when to create and export backups.
- Encrypted backup functionality helps protect exported financial data.

**Backup reminder:** Keep your encrypted backup password safe. Losing it may make your backup unrecoverable.
